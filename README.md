# XBT Self-Custody Guide

A plain, step-by-step guide to holding XBT (the BIP-110 fork of bitcoin, also called BTCB2) in your own custody, keeping it separate from your BTC.

Live site: https://rasputin-69420.github.io/xbt-guide/

Covers: splitting BTC and XBT, wallets and hardware wallets, buying XBT with BTC or dollars, consolidating, safety, sending over mesh networks, and why XBT exists.

Static HTML and CSS only, no JavaScript, no tracking. Served by GitHub Pages.

**Not financial or tax advice.** Education only. The XBT wallets and firmware mentioned are new, community-built and unaudited. Test with small amounts. Nobody needs your seed phrase.
